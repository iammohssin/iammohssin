## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I design and scale backends for **real-time, location-heavy products** — ride dispatch, delivery logistics, and conversational AI ordering. My home turf is **Node.js + TypeScript**, geospatial data, job queues, and features that survive production traffic.

- 🗣️ Built a **multilingual AI voice-ordering system** that understands **Urdu, Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi & English** (including Roman transliterations) and turns speech into structured orders, searches, and cart actions
- 🚕 Architected a **waterfall driver-dispatch engine** — sequential ride offers built on NestJS, BullMQ job queues, and MongoDB geoqueries
- 🌍 Heavy use of **geospatial queries** (MongoDB `$nearSphere`, Postgres/PostGIS) for restaurant and rider matching
- 🔧 Led a full **framework modernization**: NestJS 8 → 11, Mongoose 5 → 8, AWS SDK v2 → v3 — with a documented, low-risk upgrade path
- 💳 Production integrations: **Stripe, Twilio, Firebase, OpenAI, AWS**
- ✂️ Philosophy: minimal, surgical fixes over risky rewrites — ship small, ship safe
